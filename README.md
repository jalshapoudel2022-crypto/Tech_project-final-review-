# Study Habits and Academic Performance

## 1. About the Project

This project explores the relationship between students' study hours and their academic performance. It uses basic data analysis techniques and Python to investigate whether the number of hours students study is related to their grades.

## 2. Research Question

Is there a relationship between the number of hours students study and their academic grades?

## 3. Dataset

The dataset was obtained from Kaggle.

It contains three main variables:
- `student_id`: Unique identification number for each student.
- `study_hours`: Number of hours spent studying.
- `grade`: Academic grade obtained by the student.

A sample of 10 student records was used for this analysis.

**Dataset source:** [ https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/andreylss/study-hours-vs-grades-dataset
]

## 4. Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Google Colab
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- NumPy
- GitHub

## 5. Methods Used

The following methods were used:
- Data exploration
- Checking for missing values and duplicate records
- Calculating mean and median
- Calculating range and standard deviation
- Creating a scatter plot
- Calculating correlation

## 6. Key Findings

The analysis produced the following results:

| Measurement | Result |
|---|---:|
| Number of students | 10 |
| Average study hours | 7.17 |
| Average grade | 68.28 |
| Correlation | 0.55 |

Correlation Analysis:
The correlation coefficient for study hours and grades was   0.55, indicating a moderate positive relationship in the selected sample of 10 students. This suggests that students who studied for more hours generally tended to achieve higher grades. However, this does not prove that studying longer directly causes higher grades. Since the analysis uses only 10 students, the findings may not represent the entire dataset.

Average Analysis:
The average study time among the 10 selected students was 7.17 hours, while their average grade was 68.28 out of 100. This gives us a general overview of the study habits and academic performance within the selected sample. However, these averages only represent the 10 students included in this analysis.

Median Analysis:
The median study time was 7.275 hours, while the median grade was 65.555. This means that the middle values of the two variables were 7.275 hours and 65.555 marks. The median helps describe the typical middle value without being heavily affected by unusually high or low values.
What do these numbers mean?
Median study hours (7.275): Half of the students studied for less than or equal to 7.275 hours, while the other half studied for more than or equal to this amount.
Median grade (65.555): Half of the students received grades below or equal to 65.555, while the other half received grades above or equal to this value.


The correlation indicates a moderate positive relationship between study hours and grades in the selected sample.

## 7. Limitations

- Only 10 students were analysed.
- The dataset contains limited variables.
- Correlation does not prove causation.
- The findings may not represent the entire student population.

## 8. Conclusion

In conclusion, this project explored the relationship between study hours and academic performance using a sample of 10 students from a dataset obtained from Kaggle. The analysis showed a moderate positive correlation of 0.55 between study hours and grades.
The average study time was 7.17 hours, and the average grade was 68.28. These findings suggest that students who studied for more hours generally tended to achieve higher grades in the selected sample. However, study hours alone cannot explain academic performance, as other factors may also influence students' grades.
Overall, this project helped demonstrate how Python and statistical methods can be used to organise, analyse and interpret data.
